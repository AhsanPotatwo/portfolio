Distant Cousins

Every animal there is, and every one there ever was, comes from the same first bit of life. Start at the bottom and climb the family tree to meet over two million of your cousins.

        Read about it on Wikipedia

          Where it all comes from

          The tree is Wikidata's: every animal it knows and who its parents are, which thousands of people keep up to date. The dates come from the fossils in the Paleobiology Database, the words from Wikipedia and the photos from Wikimedia Commons, credited under each one. Scientists still argue about plenty of branches, so now and then a creature sits somewhere surprising.
