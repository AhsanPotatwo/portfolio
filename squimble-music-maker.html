Squimble Music Maker

Music that writes itself from a seed word. Pick a mood and it chooses a key, writes chord progressions and melodies, and puts together a band to play them. It uses the music theory composers have relied on for centuries, not AI, and the instruments are synthesisers built from the browser's own oscillators. Then pull it apart: change the chords, swap the instruments, redraw a melody note by note. I'm building it to score Squimble Quest.
